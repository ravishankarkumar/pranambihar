# Foods still to add to the Pranam Bihar atlas

This is a working list of food traditions that do not yet have complete coverage on the site. Existing dishes, combinations, Prasad entries and the Lai family have been removed from this backlog.

## Regional and specialty dishes

- **Sarse baingan** — mustard-based aubergine preparation from Anga
- **Bagiya** — Mithila rice-flour delicacy; decide whether it needs its own entry or should become a substantial regional branch of the existing Dal Pitha page
- **Bihari kebab** — marinated, tenderised strips of meat cooked over heat; a separate tradition from taash
- **Taash** — thin, spiced and grilled meat associated especially with Champaran; do not collapse it into Bihari kebab or ahuna meat
- **Dhuska** — fried rice-and-lentil preparation shared across the Bihar–Jharkhand food landscape
- **Bhunjia** — a family of dry sautéed vegetables, often potatoes, better documented as a technique than as one fixed recipe
- **Methauri with baingan sabzi** — preserve the combination as well as the individual preparation

## River, field and regional meat traditions

- **Roasted gaicha fish**
- **Roasted crab**
- **Roasted ghongha**
- **Bater or quail preparation**

These entries should preserve the exact local name, species or ingredient, season, cooking method and region. Where present-day harvesting or wildlife rules matter, the page should distinguish cultural memory from current availability.

## Sweets still to add

- **Kesaria peda** — the place-linked peda tradition of Kesaria, not simply saffron-flavoured *kesar peda*
- **Khurma** — especially the traditions of southwest Bihar
- **Belgrami** — associated with Udwantnagar in Bhojpur
- **Murki** — associated with Koelwar; document separately from Belgrami
- **Khurchan** — layered milk sweet associated with old Patna
- **Kala jamun and pantua** — related but distinct forms associated with Munger, Bhagalpur and Banka
- **Rabri**
- **Chhena and rasgulla variants** — define the particular Bihari forms before creating a broad entry
- **Posta-dana halwa**
- **Gaja**
