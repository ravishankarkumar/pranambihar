# Foods still to add to the Pranam Bihar atlas

## Regional and specialty dishes

- **Sarse baingan** — mustard-based aubergine preparation from Anga
- **Bagiya** — Mithila rice-flour delicacy
- **Bihari kebab or taash** — spiced mutton preparation associated with Champaran and other regions
- **Dhuska** — fried rice-and-lentil preparation
- **Bhunjia** — dry sautéed vegetables, often potatoes
- **Methauri with baingan sabzi**
- **Roasted gaicha fish**
- **Roasted crab**
- **Roasted ghongha**
- **Bater or quail preparation**
