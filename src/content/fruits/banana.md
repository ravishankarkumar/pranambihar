Banana belongs to everyday eating, market life, hospitality and ritual use. Bihar grows it at significant scale, but a useful atlas must go beyond production numbers to name the varieties people seek, the districts associated with them and the different uses of ripe and raw fruit.

Vaishali and Hajipur are widely connected with banana cultivation and trade. This entry will grow by distinguishing those local varieties rather than treating every banana as interchangeable.
