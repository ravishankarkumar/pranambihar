Bhagalpuri Zardalu places a particular mango within a particular landscape. Aroma, texture and season give the fruit its sensory identity; orchards and grower knowledge give that identity continuity.

It is one doorway into Bihar’s much larger mango culture, which also includes beloved regional varieties and changing orchard landscapes that deserve their own entries.
