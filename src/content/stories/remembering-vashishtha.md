Vashishtha Narayan Singh is remembered in Bihar through a powerful shorthand: extraordinary mathematical ability, international promise, illness and long periods outside the institutions that once recognised him.

Shorthand can preserve a name. It can also replace a person.

## What a responsible profile requires

A serious portrait must return to records rather than repeat the most dramatic version of a familiar anecdote. It should establish a clear academic timeline, speak with people who knew his work, separate documented contribution from viral claims, and handle mental illness without spectacle.

It should also ask what public remembrance is for. Is genius valuable only when it produces a celebrated theorem or prestigious appointment? What do families and institutions owe a scholar when illness changes the course of a career? Why do some stories become symbols while the underlying work remains difficult for the public to access?

## The work ahead

Pranam Bihar will develop this portrait through archival research and interviews. Until those sources are assembled, we will not use unsupported claims merely because they have been repeated often.

That approach is not less celebratory. It is a deeper form of respect: understanding a life in full, preserving what can be established, and admitting where the record is incomplete.
