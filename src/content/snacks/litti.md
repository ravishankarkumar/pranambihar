Litti can anchor a full meal with chokha, but it also works as a snack: compact, portable and filling. A single hot litti brushed with ghee may be eaten at a stall, on a journey or when a small hunger needs something more substantial than a fried bite.

The [full Litti Chokha entry](/foods/litti-chokha/) explores the complete plate. This entry preserves litti’s equally important life as a snack in its own right.
