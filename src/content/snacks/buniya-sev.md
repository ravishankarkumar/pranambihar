Here, buniya means the yellow preparation touched by chashni—not safed buniya. Salty sevai cuts through that sweetness and adds a different kind of crunch.

The balance can change handful by handful. That informality is part of the pairing: it is assembled to taste rather than governed by a fixed recipe.
