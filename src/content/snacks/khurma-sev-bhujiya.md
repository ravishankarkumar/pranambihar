Khurma brings a floury crunch and a coating of crystallised sweetness. Salty sev—also called sevai in some homes—or bhujiya answers it with salt and spice. The pleasure lies in moving between the two rather than blending them into one preparation.

The savoury accompaniment changes with what a household buys or makes. Both **khurma with sevai** and **khurma with bhujiya** belong to this snack family.
