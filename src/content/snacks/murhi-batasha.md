Murhi and batasha can be understood in more than one context. On an ordinary snack plate, the plain crisp grain makes the intense sweetness of batasha easier to carry. During Diwali Puja in many families, the same pairing becomes an offering and is shared as Prasad.

The food does not change identity by ingredient alone; occasion, preparation and intention matter.
