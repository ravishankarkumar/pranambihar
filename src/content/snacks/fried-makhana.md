Heat transforms makhana from pale and yielding to crisp. Some homes use ghee, others oil; seasoning may stop at salt or extend to pepper, chilli and spice blends.

Its place in Bihar’s snack culture also connects the plate to ponds, cultivation, processing and the labour of the makhana-growing regions.
