Samosa is one of Bihar’s most visible everyday snacks. It may arrive by itself beside chai, under chutney, or opened beneath a serving of ghugni. Sweet shops fry it through the day; roadside and station stalls make it part of the rhythm of travel.

The filling, pastry, size and accompaniments differ from town to town. Its popularity across Bihar matters more than claiming one version as the state’s standard.
