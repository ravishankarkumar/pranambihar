Aloo chap is built for the moment it leaves the oil: hot outside, soft and seasoned within. Shape, coating, filling and the chutney served beside it differ between vendors and homes.

It belongs to Bihar’s everyday street-food vocabulary without needing one standardised recipe to represent every town.
