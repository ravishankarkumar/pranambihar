Ghugni supplies warmth, spice and substance; chura keeps the plate light and catches some of its masala. The chana, consistency, garnish and proportions shift between regions and homes.

This is both a complete combination and an everyday snack. Its importance lies in the way the two foods are eaten together, not merely in either recipe alone.
