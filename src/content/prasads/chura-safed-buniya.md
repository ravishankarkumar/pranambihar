Here, *safed buniya* means the dry white sugar buniya also known as sugar boondi. It is distinct from the yellow buniya soaked in chashni that can be pressed into laddus. Families carry the combination home from Kanwar journeys and religious sites, with routes and shrine customs varying across Bihar.
