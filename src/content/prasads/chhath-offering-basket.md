The daura or soop is not a fixed shopping checklist. Families arrange offerings according to region, season, household practice and availability. Gagar nimbu, sugarcane, coconut, bananas, oranges and other fruits may sit alongside Thekua, batashe, khaja and further local offerings. The basket, its careful arrangement and the collective labour of carrying it to the water are part of the prasad tradition.
