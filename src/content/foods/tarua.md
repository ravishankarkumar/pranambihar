Tarua is not one vegetable but a method and a place on the table. Seasonal produce is sliced, seasoned, coated and fried, creating crisp edges and a tender centre.

A meal may include tarua made from potato, aubergine, pumpkin, pointed gourd or another locally available vegetable. Documenting the range means following the agricultural calendar as much as the recipe.
