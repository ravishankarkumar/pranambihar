Khaja builds texture from repeated layers of dough and fat before frying and sweetening. The result can be crisp, light and flaky, breaking into fine sheets rather than behaving like a dense sweet.

Silao in Nalanda district is particularly associated with the form. A future maker story will document how its layers are built, how weather changes the work and how producers understand the relationship between place and reputation.
