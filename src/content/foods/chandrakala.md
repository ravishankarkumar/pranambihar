Chandrakala or pedakiya encloses a sweet filling—often khoya, coconut, cardamom and dry fruit—inside pastry before frying and sweetening.

Names, shapes and whether syrup is used vary. Those differences connect the sweet to family and festival practice rather than diminishing its identity.
