Chandrakala encloses a sweet filling—often khoya, coconut, cardamom and dry fruit—inside pastry before frying and finishing with syrup.

It is sold across Bihar. Although it belongs to the same broad family of filled pastries as pirukya, the names should not be collapsed: Chandrakala is recognised through its own shape and syrup-finished character.
