Bihari-style chaat is better understood as a family than one fixed recipe. Depending on the town and vendor, a plate may begin with kachori, ghugni, singhada or chura before adding curd, chutneys, onion, spices and something crisp.

The regional character often comes from the base and the balance: warm pulses against cool curd, mustard-oil sharpness, sweet and sour chutneys, and crunch added immediately before serving.
