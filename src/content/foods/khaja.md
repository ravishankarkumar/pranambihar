Khaja should not be reduced to a substitute for Silao Khaja. Across Bihar, halwais make layered sweets under the name in different shapes, textures and degrees of syrupiness. Silao represents a protected, place-specific craft; this entry provides a home for the wider family, including traditions associated with places such as Pipra.
