Bel sharbat is prepared when the hard-shelled fruit is ripe. Its aromatic pulp is separated from the seeds and fibres, loosened with water and usually sweetened before serving.

## A household summer drink

The thickness, sweetness and additions vary between homes. Some preparations remain pulpy and substantial, while others are strained into a lighter drink. Jaggery, sugar, black salt or other seasonings may be used according to family preference.

Bel belongs to a wider seasonal knowledge of fruit, heat and refreshment in Bihar. Documenting the drink properly means recording how families identify ripe fruit, extract the pulp and adjust the final sharbat by taste rather than presenting one recipe as definitive.
