Balushahi is shaped from flour and fat, fried slowly and glazed with sugar. Its appeal comes from a surface that gives way to a layered, slightly crumbly interior.

Versions appear across northern India. Bihar’s atlas should focus on locally reputed centres and makers rather than claiming the entire form for one place.
