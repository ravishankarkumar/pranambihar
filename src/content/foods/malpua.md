Malpua is made from a batter that may include flour, milk, banana and aromatics before being fried and sweetened. The edge, centre and syrup each contribute a different texture.

Recipes travel across eastern and northern South Asia, and Bihar’s versions belong within that wider family. Household proportions and festival associations are more useful than an unsupported claim to one definitive origin.
