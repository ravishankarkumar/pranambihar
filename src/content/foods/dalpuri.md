Dalpuri encloses a seasoned pulse filling inside wheat dough before cooking. It belongs to a broad family of filled breads, but the grain, pulse texture, spice and serving tradition give each regional version its character.

Some tables pair it with vegetable dishes; others place it beside kheer or festive accompaniments. The pairing is often as important as the bread itself.
