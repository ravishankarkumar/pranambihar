Barh ki lai is associated with the town of Barh in Patna district. Puffed or expanded seeds are brought together with khoya and sugar into a clustered ball or disc.

Its structure—light grains held by a richer binding—sets it apart from dense milk sweets and makes the technique worth documenting closely.
