Often called ahuna or handi meat, this preparation is associated with the Champaran region. Meat, mustard oil, garlic and spices cook together in an earthen vessel whose opening is sealed while heat works slowly inside.

The technique is more important than a dramatic list of ingredients. A sealed pot retains moisture and aroma; patient cooking gives the spices and meat time to become one sauce.

## A name that travelled

Restaurants far beyond Champaran now use the name. Future Pranam Bihar reporting will trace the people, roadside kitchens and commercial adaptations that carried the dish outward—and ask how the regional identity is represented along the way.
