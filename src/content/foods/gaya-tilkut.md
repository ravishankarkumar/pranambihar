Tilkut is a winter sweet built around sesame. The seed is processed and combined with sugar or jaggery, then worked into a compact disc whose aroma and brittle texture depend on careful roasting and pounding.

Gaya is widely associated with tilkut, although the sweet appears elsewhere and varies by maker. Its season matters: sesame, winter markets and festival calendars are part of the experience.
