Dahi chura combines softened beaten rice with yoghurt and a sweetener such as jaggery or sugar. Banana and other accompaniments may join the bowl, but its appeal rests in the direct meeting of grain, dairy and sweetness.

It is closely associated with winter and Makar Sankranti, while remaining an everyday breakfast in many homes. The ratio, texture and accompanying foods vary widely.
