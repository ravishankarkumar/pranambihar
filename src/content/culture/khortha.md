Khortha is strongly associated with Jharkhand and the Chota Nagpur plateau, but its language geography also reaches into the far south of Bihar. It is heard in areas of Aurangabad, Gaya, Nawada and Jamui adjoining Jharkhand.

## Bihar's borders are not language borders

Khortha makes visible a recurring truth about Bihar's linguistic landscape: state lines and speech communities do not perfectly coincide. Work, kinship, trade and movement connect speakers across the border.

## What deserves documentation

Future reporting should record how Khortha speakers in Bihar describe their language, how it interacts with Magahi and Hindi, and where it appears in songs, performance, schooling and public communication. Bihar's language story is incomplete if it only describes languages located entirely within the state.
