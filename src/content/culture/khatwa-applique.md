Khatwa builds images and patterns by placing shaped pieces of cloth onto a ground fabric and securing them with stitch. Material reuse, colour decisions, cutting skill and the labour of assembly are central to the work.

The craft has a registered geographical indication in Bihar. Product diversification should be documented alongside the maker groups and working arrangements that sustain it.
