Maithili is spoken across the Mithila region of north Bihar and the eastern Terai of Nepal. It belongs simultaneously to household conversation, literary traditions, music and a cultural geography that crosses the international border.

## Constitutional recognition

Maithili is included in the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution of India. That recognition gives it a distinct place in national institutional life, although census totals may still understate its reach when speakers report Hindi instead of their home language.

## Tirhuta, Kaithi and Devanagari

Tirhuta was historically a principal script for Maithili, and regional forms of Kaithi were also used. Devanagari is the script most commonly encountered today. These scripts should not be treated as decorative relics: manuscripts, family documents and lettering practices preserve different chapters of the language's written history.
