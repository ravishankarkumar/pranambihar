Tharu languages are spoken by Tharu communities across the Terai of Nepal and adjoining regions of India. In Bihar, the language is associated particularly with northern areas of East and West Champaran near the Nepal border.

## A cross-border language world

The Tharu linguistic landscape extends beyond Bihar and contains internal variation. Its relationships with neighbouring Indo-Aryan languages are complex, and broad labels can hide differences between communities and places.

## Documenting with the community

Future reporting should connect language with speakers' accounts of land, forest, movement, memory and contemporary life. It should also ask how schooling, migration and dominant languages affect transmission between generations. The community's own names and distinctions must guide that work.
