Bajjika is spoken across a broad part of north-western Bihar, especially around Muzaffarpur, Vaishali, Sitamarhi, Sheohar and Samastipur. It is also heard in adjoining parts of Darbhanga and across the border in Nepal. The name *Tirhutiya* is sometimes encountered because much of this area falls within the historic Tirhut region.

## A language obscured by larger labels

The number of Bajjika speakers is difficult to establish. Census returns have often absorbed regional languages into Hindi, while speakers themselves may use different answers at home and in formal settings. A language atlas therefore has to look beyond a single population figure and ask where, when and with whom Bajjika is spoken.

## What this page should preserve

Bajjika lives through conversation, humour, songs, proverbs and everyday storytelling. Future reporting should follow speakers across villages and cities, document differences within the region, and listen to how younger generations use the language in education, media and digital life.
