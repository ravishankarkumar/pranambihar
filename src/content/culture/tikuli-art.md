Tikuli refers locally to the decorative dot worn between the eyebrows. The associated craft has moved through changing materials and markets while retaining a strong connection to Patna’s artisan neighbourhoods.

The contemporary form commonly uses painted board surfaces. Its story is therefore one of both continuity and reinvention.
