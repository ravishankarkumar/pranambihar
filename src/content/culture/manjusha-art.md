Manjusha art connects painting, storytelling and festival practice in the Anga region. Its recognisable borders, figures and narrative sequences belong to a wider living tradition, not simply a graphic style.

This entry will grow around practising artists, regional vocabulary and the ways the form is taught, commissioned and adapted today.
