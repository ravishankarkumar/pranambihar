Surjapuri is spoken across the Purnia division—Araria, Purnia, Katihar and Kishanganj—and in adjoining areas of West Bengal. Its geography belongs to a multilingual borderland connected with Nepal and Bangladesh as well as Bihar and Bengal.

## Beyond the label “Hindi”

Surjapuri has often been grouped under Hindi in census reporting. Linguistically, however, it is associated more closely with the Kamta group, including Rajbanshi and Rangpuri, and it blends westward with eastern varieties of Maithili.

## Listening to the borderland

This page should grow through speakers' own descriptions of identity and language. Markets, migration, songs, marriage networks and local media can reveal connections that a state-by-state map misses. Surjapuri belongs at the centre of any account of north-eastern Bihar.
