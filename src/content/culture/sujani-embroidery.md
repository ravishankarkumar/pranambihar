Sujani joins layers of cloth through stitch, often creating figurative or geometric narratives across the surface. Domestic reuse, care and visual storytelling meet in the textile.

Its contemporary life also raises questions about authorship, collectives, livelihoods and who receives credit when handmade traditions reach wider markets.
