Vidyapati’s poetry belongs both to manuscripts and to living performance. His work moved across courtly, devotional and intimate registers, and its influence extended beyond the political borders of present-day Bihar.

The continuing life of the songs—in voices, ceremonies and adaptations—is as important as the historical biography.
