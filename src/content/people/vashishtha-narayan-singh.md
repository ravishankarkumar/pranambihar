Vashishtha Narayan Singh’s life is frequently compressed into claims of genius and tragedy. A responsible portrait should instead distinguish documented scholarship from folklore and treat his mental illness without spectacle.

His story also opens a wider conversation about institutions, care, recognition and Bihar’s scientific aspirations.
