Sher Shah Suri’s career rose from the politics of Bihar into control of a large North Indian empire. Administrative reputation and later legend have made him a recurring reference point in accounts of roads, currency and revenue.

His tomb at Sasaram brings the imperial story back to a specific landscape of water, stone and memory.
