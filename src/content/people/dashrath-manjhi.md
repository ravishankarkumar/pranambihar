Dashrath Manjhi’s persistence made him an emblem of individual resolve. But the mountain cannot be separated from the social geography around it: distance from services, caste, poverty and the uneven value assigned to rural lives.

The strongest telling honours the labour without turning structural neglect into an inspirational backdrop.
