Champaran is often remembered through Gandhi’s arrival in 1917. The fuller history begins with cultivators, coercive indigo arrangements, local organisers and the collection of testimony that made exploitation publicly legible.

The movement also connected protest with education, sanitation and institution-building. Its importance belongs to collective action rather than to one heroic visit alone.
