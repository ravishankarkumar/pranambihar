On 22 March 1912, Bihar and Orissa were constituted as a province separate from Bengal. Patna became the administrative centre, and new legislative, judicial and archival institutions emerged over the following years.

This was not simply a line drawn on a map. It followed political demands and produced new arguments about language, representation, capital and regional identity.
