Vaishali is frequently introduced as an early republic. The phrase is useful only when handled carefully: its assemblies and confederated structures belonged to their own social and political world, not to a modern electoral state.

The deeper story is more interesting than the slogan. It asks how collective decisions were organised, who participated and why the political memory of the Licchavis remained influential.
