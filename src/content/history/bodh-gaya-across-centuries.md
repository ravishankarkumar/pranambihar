Bodh Gaya is simultaneously a local place and a global sacred geography. The Mahabodhi landscape records layers of construction, restoration, devotion and contest across centuries.

Its history is not complete without pilgrims, monastic communities and the continuing relationship between the temple complex and the wider town.
