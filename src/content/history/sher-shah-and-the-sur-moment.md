Sher Shah Suri’s reign was short, but the Sur state became important to later accounts of revenue, roads, communication and imperial authority. Sasaram anchors this large political story in a specific Bihar landscape.

The monumental tomb is an entry point—not the whole account. The history also needs the conflicts, institutions and people through which power was exercised.
