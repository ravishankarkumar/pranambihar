Magadha’s story begins with geography but cannot be explained by geography alone. River routes, fertile land, access to resources and ambitious political organisation helped rulers based first around Rajagriha and later Pataliputra build influence far beyond the region.

The name gathers several dynastic chapters rather than one uninterrupted empire. Reading them together reveals why Bihar became central to arguments about statecraft, urban life and authority in early South Asia.
